## Reading

1. **NVFP4 wins on almost everything.** Decode ×1 ~2.4× the FP8 and ~+10% over EXL3; prefill on par
   with FP8 and ~+65% over EXL3; best c4 concurrency.
2. **Upstream FP8 is the slowest in decode** (49-50 tok/s, -60% vs NVFP4): it uses the integrated
   MTP k=3 instead of the DFlash2 k=7 drafter of the other two — fewer speculative tokens accepted per step.
   Prefill, on the other hand, holds up very well (~2100, on par with NVFP4).
3. **FP8 is however the most reliable on needle retrieval**: 3/3 and 2/2 in both passes, the only one
   that never missed. Consistent with it being the least aggressive quantisation (~306 GiB).
4. **EXL3** remains interesting only for sustained long-decode (100.7 tok/s, +6% over NVFP4) and
   for the claimed quality (KLD 0.0246 vs 0.0605 NVFP4 — MiaAI figure, not verified in house),
   but it pays a clean -40% on prefill.

## Methodology notes

- FP8 boot: 1236 s, CUDA graphs captured, neither of the two known failure modes
  (`fp8_ds_mla` / `persistent_topk`) — patches and `sm121-v8` image correct.
- zai-org weights: drop-in verified (arch `Glm5NextForConditionalGeneration`,
  `quantization_config` fp8 e4m3 dynamic, chat template, vision tower present).
- The historical `gate.md` baseline remains unreliable: use only these numbers.
- c4 variance on EXL3 and FP8 (stragglers with high TTFT on individual streams) — NVFP4 the most stable.
