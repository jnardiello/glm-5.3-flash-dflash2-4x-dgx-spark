## Asterisks (harness bugs at the time of measurement — fix in progress)

1. **Undersized prefill**: calibration 12.97 tok/repeat vs ~9.04 actual → effective prompts of ~20.9k and ~69.5k
   tokens. The tok/s figures are valid for the real sizes (prompt_tokens/TTFT) but not size-for-size vs gate.md (@31k).
2. **@1400 is actually ~404 tokens**: the structured prompt terminates naturally (finish_reason=stop, 404 tokens in
   every run) — it is not a sustained 1400-token decode.
3. **Needle miss = truncation**: every needle_ok=false had finish_reason=length (64-token cap); the runs that
   reached stop were 3/3 needle_ok=true. A retrieval failure cannot be distinguished from a truncated answer.
4. **Prose variance**: 34.6 → 29.7 between two passes 16 minutes apart, same configuration.

## Note on the historical baseline

The baseline in `docs/gate.md:75-87` (prefill ~2356, decode 44/39.9) is **underestimated on decode**: measured under
different conditions, not with this harness. Real structured decode ≈ 2× the historical value. For the 3-variant
verdict this file + the JSONs are authoritative, not gate.md.
